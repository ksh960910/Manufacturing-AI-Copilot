# Etching Process Inspection Guideline

## Purpose

This guideline supports investigation of PCB defects associated with copper
removal, including mouse bites, open circuits, spurs, and spurious copper.

## Process Parameters to Review

- Etchant concentration and replenishment records.
- Etchant temperature and solution condition.
- Conveyor speed and dwell time in the etching chamber.
- Spray pressure, nozzle condition, and coverage uniformity.
- Photoresist coating, exposure, development, and adhesion results.

## Investigation Procedure

1. Identify the affected lot, panel, and production time window.
2. Compare the defect pattern with boards produced before and after the lot.
3. Review recorded process parameters against the approved process window.
4. Inspect photoresist artifacts and copper edge quality under magnification.
5. Quarantine material when the defect affects conductor width, spacing, or
   electrical continuity.

## Corrective Action Guidance

1. Correct any parameter outside the approved process window.
2. Replace or replenish process chemicals according to the operating procedure.
3. Clean or maintain spray nozzles and conveyor equipment when coverage is
   uneven.
4. Verify the result using a representative first-article panel before normal
   production resumes.

## Quality Record

Record the lot identifier, observed defect, measured process values, corrective
action, verification result, and approval decision in the quality record.
