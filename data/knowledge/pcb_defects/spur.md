# Spur

## Severity

Medium

## Description

A spur is a small, unwanted copper protrusion extending from a trace. It can
reduce spacing to nearby conductors and may develop into a short-circuit risk.

## Likely Causes

- Incomplete etching.
- Photoresist residue or an imaging artifact.
- Process variation in etchant condition or conveyor speed.
- Copper debris remaining after processing.

## Inspection Procedure

1. Inspect the spur at magnification and measure its spacing from nearby
   conductors.
2. Check the surrounding area for similar residual copper features.
3. Compare the observed spacing with the approved design rule or tolerance.
4. Review etching and photoresist process records for the lot.

## Recommended Actions

1. Review the etching process and confirm complete copper removal.
2. Inspect photoresist coating, exposure, and development quality.
3. Remove residual copper only through an approved rework procedure.
4. Reinspect the lot for similar features, especially in fine-pitch regions.

## Shipment Decision

Hold boards where the spur violates the required conductor spacing. Boards
within spacing tolerance require documented inspection review before release.
