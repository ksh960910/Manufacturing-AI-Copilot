# Mouse Bite

## Severity

Medium

## Description

A mouse bite is irregular erosion along the edge of a copper trace or pad. It
reduces the effective conductor width and can weaken electrical reliability.

## Likely Causes

- Over-etching during PCB manufacturing.
- Excessive etchant concentration or an out-of-control chemical condition.
- Uneven etching time, temperature, or conveyor speed.
- Defective or poorly adhered photoresist.

## Inspection Procedure

1. Inspect the affected trace at magnification and compare its remaining width
   with the manufacturing tolerance.
2. Check adjacent traces and pads for similar edge erosion.
3. Review etchant concentration, temperature, conveyor speed, and dwell-time
   records for the affected lot.
4. Examine the photoresist pattern for lifting, pinholes, or incomplete cover.

## Recommended Actions

1. Review and correct the etching-process parameters.
2. Verify chemical concentration and replenish or replace etchant if needed.
3. Inspect photoresist coating and imaging quality.
4. Reinspect the affected lot, focusing on fine-pitch traces.

## Shipment Decision

Do not release boards with trace width below the approved tolerance. Boards
within tolerance require documented engineering review before release.
