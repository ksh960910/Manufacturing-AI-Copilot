# Short Circuit

## Severity

Critical

## Description

A short circuit is an unintended electrical connection between conductive
traces, pads, or copper areas. It can cause malfunction, overheating, or
failure during electrical test.

## Likely Causes

- Insufficient etching that left a copper bridge.
- Copper bridge formation between adjacent traces.
- Conductive contamination, debris, or plating residue.
- Imaging or photoresist defects in fine-pitch areas.

## Inspection Procedure

1. Perform isolation or resistance testing between the affected nets.
2. Inspect the suspected bridge under magnification.
3. Check adjacent fine-pitch features for additional unintended connections.
4. Review etching and cleaning records for the affected production lot.

## Recommended Actions

1. Place the affected board and lot on hold immediately.
2. Remove residual conductive material only through an approved rework process.
3. Reinspect nearby traces and repeat electrical isolation testing.
4. Review etching, cleaning, and photoresist controls before production resumes.

## Shipment Decision

Do not ship the affected board. Release requires passing electrical isolation
testing and quality approval after the root cause has been addressed.
