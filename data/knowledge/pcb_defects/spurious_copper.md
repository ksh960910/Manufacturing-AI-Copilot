# Spurious Copper

## Severity

Medium

## Description

Spurious copper is unwanted, isolated copper remaining on the PCB surface. It
may create a contamination risk, reduce clearance, or form an unintended
electrical connection after assembly.

## Likely Causes

- Incomplete copper removal during etching.
- Photoresist defect, pinhole, or poor adhesion.
- Etching-process variation or exhausted chemicals.
- Copper debris transferred during handling.

## Inspection Procedure

1. Inspect the isolated copper feature and measure its clearance to nearby
   conductive areas.
2. Check similar board regions for recurring residual copper.
3. Review photoresist integrity and etching-process records.
4. Verify that the feature is not part of the approved copper artwork.

## Recommended Actions

1. Inspect etching quality and restore process settings if needed.
2. Check photoresist integrity before the next production run.
3. Remove residual copper using an approved rework method.
4. Perform optical inspection on boards from the affected lot.

## Shipment Decision

Hold boards with spurious copper that violates clearance requirements or cannot
be safely removed. Release requires inspection evidence and quality approval.
